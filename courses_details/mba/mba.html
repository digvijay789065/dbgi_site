Master of Business Administration (MBA)

Shape your future with our comprehensive MBA program designed to develop business leaders and entrepreneurs

About the Program

Department of Management

The MBA program at DBGI Saharanpur is a 2-year postgraduate degree program approved by AICTE, Ministry of HRD Govt. of India & Affiliated to Mahamaya Technical University, Lucknow.

Our program is designed to equip students with the knowledge, skills, and strategic perspectives essential to business leadership and organizational success in today's competitive global environment.

With a perfect blend of theoretical knowledge and practical exposure, our MBA program prepares students to take on challenging roles in the corporate world.

Program Details

Eligibility

  • Graduation in any stream with minimum 50% aggregate
  • 45% for SC/ST candidates
  • Valid CMAT score conducted by AICTE, New Delhi
  • MAT/CAT scores are also acceptable

Duration

  • 2-year full-time program
  • 4 semesters
  • Industry internship included
  • Project work in final semester

Admission Procedure

  • Admission based on valid CMAT/MAT/CAT score
  • 85% general seats + 15% management quota
  • Merit-based counseling organized by the institute
  • Personal interview for final selection

Specializations

Marketing

Focus on consumer behavior, brand management, and digital marketing strategies

Finance

Learn financial analysis, investment strategies, and corporate finance management

Human Resource Management

Develop skills in talent acquisition, organizational behavior, and HR strategy

Information Technology

Combine business management with IT systems and digital transformation

Documents Required

  • Proof of Age
  • Matriculation Worksheet and Certificate
  • Intermediate Worksheet and Certificate
  • Aadhar Card
  • Transfer/Migration Certificate
  • Character Certificate
  • Proof of Caste Certificate
  • 10 recent passport size photographs
  • Valid CMAT/MAT/CAT Score card

About the Program

Department of Management

The BBA program at DBGI Saharanpur is a 3-year undergraduate degree program designed to provide students with a strong foundation in business principles and management practices.

Our program focuses on developing essential business skills, critical thinking abilities, and leadership qualities needed to succeed in today's dynamic business environment.

With a curriculum that balances theoretical knowledge with practical applications, our BBA program prepares students for successful careers in business or for pursuing higher studies like MBA.

Program Details

Eligibility

  • 10+2 in any stream with minimum 50% aggregate
  • 45% for SC/ST candidates
  • Direct admission based on merit
  • Personal interview may be conducted

Duration

  • 3-year full-time program
  • 6 semesters
  • Summer internship after 4th semester
  • Project work in final semester

Admission Procedure

  • Direct admission based on 10+2 marks
  • Merit-based selection process
  • 85% general seats + 15% management quota
  • Counseling and document verification

Specializations

Marketing

Learn marketing principles, consumer behavior, and sales management techniques

Finance

Understand financial management, accounting principles, and investment basics

Human Resources

Develop skills in recruitment, training, and employee relations management

International Business

Explore global business practices, trade policies, and cross-cultural management

Documents Required

  • Proof of Age
  • Matriculation Worksheet and Certificate
  • Intermediate Worksheet and Certificate
  • Aadhar Card
  • Transfer/Migration Certificate
  • Character Certificate
  • Proof of Caste Certificate
  • 10 recent passport size photographs
  • 10+2 Mark Sheet and Certificate
